🛡️ Secure Connection

This bridge page facilitates a secure connection between your hardware wallet and BlockWallet. Your private keys remain safely on your hardware device at all times.

📋 Connection Steps

Please follow these steps to establish a secure connection:

  1. Connect your hardware wallet to your computer using the USB cable
  2. Unlock your hardware wallet using your PIN or password
  3. Navigate to and open the Ethereum application on your device
  4. When prompted by your browser, select your device and allow the connection
This page will automatically navigate to account selection once your device is connected.
If you experience issues, ensure your hardware wallet firmware is up to date.
